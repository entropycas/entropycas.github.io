MEDesign

As a team effort we are in the development stage of a medical device for pediatric asthma patients. This project is currently ongoing, so this page is subject to change as we make progress.

Healthcare Sensors Prototyping

Project Overview

Our team goal is to create a low-cost, easy-to-use device that helps monitor and manage asthma symptoms in children. We are focusing on developing a wearable sensor that tracks respiratory patterns and potentially environmental factors that may trigger asthma attacks. The device is in the design process and we are validating that specific sensors would be useful in supporting patients.
